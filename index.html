02 · Fixed in advance, not revisable

The bar the strategy has to clear

All six conditions, on forward records only — never a backtest. Any change to this list is logged with its date, its reason, and how far the strategy was from clearing the bar at that moment — the field that separates an honest revision from a convenient one.

  1. At least 100 forward decisions. Not 8, not 20. Small samples produce confident-looking numbers that mean nothing.
  2. Net expectancy positive after real fees, with the lower bound of a bootstrap confidence interval above zero — not merely a positive point estimate.
  3. Still clears condition 2 with the single largest contributor removed. An edge carried by one trade is one trade, not an edge. Stated as a share of P&L this test breaks when total P&L is near zero, so it is run by deletion instead.
  4. Measured against realised reward-to-risk, not designed. Rules with a time limit let winners close early; the breakeven win rate moves accordingly.
  5. At least 25 decisions in each of two different volatility bands, spanning six months. Bands are pre-calibrated 4-hour true-range cut-points, so a different regime is computed rather than declared — it is otherwise the softest word on this list.
  6. Annualised Sharpe on daily net returns of at least 1.0. An absolute floor, not a comparison against the live account: its Sharpe is computed by Binance under a formula we do not have, and two Sharpes computed by different parties under different conventions cannot be subtracted from one another.
Current status

Not met. Nothing is for sale. The full readout against all six conditions — every number produced by the measurement script, dated — is published at the moment the bar is cleared and before anything is sold, so the claim can never be made without the figures behind it. Work in progress toward the bar is not broadcast while it is in progress; the commitment is the publication, not the running commentary.

Counted cumulatively from the strategy version start, never as a trailing window — a trailing window would let us simply wait for a good hundred. Changing the strategy starts a new version at zero. These conditions bind us, as sample one; traders who join later set their own terms and publish their own records.

What the numbers above do not say

Read this
  • Binance tags this account HIGH LEVERAGE. Our own published research finds that beyond roughly 3× leverage, even a genuinely positive edge compounds into losses, and that leverage adds no information at all — it scales mean and standard deviation together. That tension is ours to answer, and we would rather state it than have you find it.
  • 190 days is one market regime. Condition 5 of the bar exists because of exactly this.
  • Aggregate copier P&L is not any individual's result — entry timing, size and settings all differ. Past results do not predict future ones, and copy trading futures can lose the entire amount committed.
