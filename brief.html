01 · What is in one

Two sections, and the second one is labelled

Facts

required
  • What moved, what launched, what settled — stated as events, with sources attached where a source exists.
  • A brief cannot be published here without this section.

Reading

no predictive weight
  • What the facts might mean, kept apart from the facts and marked every time it is shown.
  • Optional. A brief with facts and no reading is a complete brief.
Why this is a field and not a writing style

Facts and reading arrive as two separate fields and are stored as two separate fields. A promise that is the shape of the data cannot be broken without the publish being rejected.
